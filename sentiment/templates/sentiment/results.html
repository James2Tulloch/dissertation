This page shows daily average sentiment and a 7-day rolling average, plus an optional OLS trend line in a second chart. An OLS trend line is a best-fit straight line that tries to show the general direction of your data. In a time-series, it can reveal whether sentiment (or any other metric) is drifting upward or downward. Large day-to-day changes are annotated with arrows labeled Δ=±X.
